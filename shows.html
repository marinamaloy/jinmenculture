行业背景与参考资料

INDUSTRY CONTEXT & SOURCES

2025年全国沉浸式演艺专项报告显示，中国沉浸式演艺总票房规模接近20亿元，观演人数突破1200万人次；25至35岁是消费主力。

A 2025 national report placed China's immersive performing-arts box office near RMB 2 billion with more than 12 million visits; audiences aged 25–35 formed the core consumer group.

— Guangming Daily

上海市文化和旅游局报道显示，截至2022年12月，上海版《不眠之夜》已演出超过1500场，累计观众超过45万人次，显示上海沉浸式现场娱乐具有成熟受众基础。

The Shanghai culture authority reported that the Shanghai production of Sleep No More had exceeded 1,500 performances and 450,000 visitors by December 2022, demonstrating an established local audience for immersive live entertainment.

— 上海市文化和旅游局
