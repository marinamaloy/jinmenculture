About the Show

About the Show

Step inside an old theatre, where the evening takes an unexpected turn.

As the show begins, one of the performers suddenly dies. The theatre owner, an illusionist, sets out to uncover the truth behind the mysterious death. Magic, theatre and suspense come together in a 90-minute story set against the atmosphere of a vintage circus.

Guests remain seated in the auditorium. At selected moments, they speak with the characters, answer questions and take part in the magic — becoming witnesses to the story as the mystery unfolds.

English translation · 蓝胖儿

Ghost of the Circus was so good!

It was not just thrilling and exciting — it was also romantic and warm.

Read full translation · Story spoilers

A breathtaking performance!

First of all, the stage design was wonderfully clever. The dim lighting, mysterious scenery and ghost effects that appeared from time to time made it feel as though we had entered a world that was both frightening and romantic. I was completely drawn into the atmosphere, as if I had become part of the story myself.

Secondly, the performers were outstanding. They had to handle high-altitude, high-speed stunts while coordinating with the ghost effects, showing remarkable professionalism and courage. The moment at the climax when the performers plunged from a height into water left me stunned!

Finally, the story was captivating too. Although ghosts were its theme, it was full of warmth and emotion. The courage, wisdom and love the performers conveyed really moved me.

Especially at the end, when the ghosts turned into light and disappeared into the night sky, I felt the beauty and hope of life.

Overall, Ghost of the Circus is a performance I would highly recommend. It was not just thrilling and exciting — it was also romantic and warm.

Ghost of the Circus — who do you think won?

I loved circuses as a child, but as I have grown older, I have become increasingly afraid of them. They seem to be places where joy and pain always coexist. Before coming, I was told that, because of the nature of the performance, late arrival was not allowed. That, along with the title, made me especially curious and excited.

They have to act, dance and perform magic. Add a completely dark theatre, and even hitting the right positions becomes a challenge. I cannot imagine how many things they have to keep track of at once. Respect!

The theatre is at Star Space on the seventh floor of the east wing of Shimao Plaza — an excellent location.

With the added Halloween atmosphere, it made for a thoroughly enjoyable evening.

English translation

“Some wounds become another eye”

“Some wounds never scab over. They become another eye, watching over the people we love, forever.”

Read full translation · Story spoilers

When Chris lit the last kerosene lamp in the abandoned circus, I had to pinch the back of my hand to stop myself from crying out loud. This once-dazzling magician was now setting up a séance with trembling hands, just to say sorry to Angela, who had died.

The shattering beauty of magical realism:

  • A rust-covered carousel transformed into a time machine through projections.
  • Playing cards drifting down from above suddenly froze into Angela's silhouette.
  • When Chris cried out, “I would rather be trapped in that night,” the chandelier shattered in response.

Every detail tugged at my nerves.

As Chris repeated the rope movements of his escape act, the lights cast Angela's dancing shadow on the wall. In the memory of their first successful two-person levitation, ribbons drifted down, mingling with tonight's rain… Sobs could be heard all around the theatre. The woman in the front row had already soaked through three tissues.

Looking at the neon lights of East Nanjing Road after the show, I suddenly remembered that line:

“Some wounds never scab over. They become another eye, watching over the people we love, forever.”

Producer Model

The producer handles venue operations, marketing and ticketing; Jinmen Culture provides the show content. Suggested revenue split: 55% to the producer and 45% to Jinmen Culture as the content partner. Terms are open to discussion.

Investor Model

Investment: RMB 400,000–560,000. The current proposal gives the investor 35% of net profit until cumulative distributions reach twice the invested amount, followed by an ongoing 15% share of net profit. We are open to discussing alternative terms.

Venue Partnership

A venue partner provides space and reduced rent in exchange for a share of ticket revenue. Suitable for performance venues and commercial spaces. Rent, revenue share and the partnership structure are agreed together.

What is the current project budget?

The current illustrative start-up production budget is RMB 400,000–560,000. The final budget must be confirmed for the partnership model, venue, production scope and city.

02

When could the production break even?

The 5–12 week range is a preliminary scenario, not a fixed payback period or a return guarantee. The example uses eight shows per week and 70% occupancy; actual results also depend on venue capacity, average ticket price and costs. We will refine the budget and model together once a venue is agreed.

03

How long does production take?

The current estimated production period is approximately two to three months, including script confirmation, illusion and set adaptation, rehearsals, technical tests and previews.

04

What kind of venue is required?

The preliminary concept is designed for approximately 200–300 m², a ceiling height above four metres and 100–150 guests. Final requirements follow a technical site survey.
