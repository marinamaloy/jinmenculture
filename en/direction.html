04 · CREATIVE DIRECTION

Shanghai Show Creative Direction & Stage Illusion Design

Every Great Show Deserves One Impossible Moment

A Director's Vision

In a world saturated with digital effects, what truly gives audiences goosebumps is not a CGI spectacle on a screen — it is a real person, standing in front of you, doing something that should not be physically possible.

Wang Su has spent 18 years engineering exactly those moments. As a creative director, he understands how to locate the precise point in a production's narrative arc where the "impossible" will hit hardest — and then make that impossibility happen live on stage. The result: audiences remember not the lights, not the set — only the moment that stopped their breath.

What He Brings to Your Production

🎩

Illusion Narrative Design

Designing illusion sequences from a storytelling perspective — not where to put a trick, but how that impossible moment serves and amplifies the emotional arc.

🎥

Large-Scale Show Creative Direction

From concept to curtain call — creative conceptualization, visual identity, scene design, and maintaining the balance between artistic vision and technical execution.

⚙

In-House Engineering Advantage

Cross-Discipline Creative Experience

From immersive theatre to major TV variety shows (SMG's City of Musicals), from brand activations to residency productions — Wang Su's creative language delivers impact across every format.

COLLABORATION GUIDE

Before We Begin,
Questions & Answers.

Project types, scope, production method and quotations.

01

Which projects suit this service?

Concerts, tours, brand launches, corporate galas, television, cultural festivals, tourism productions and original immersive projects.

02

Can you direct only one key scene?

Yes. Scope can range from one illusion sequence or consultancy to the visual concept and illusion direction of a complete production.

Do you also design and build the apparatus?
