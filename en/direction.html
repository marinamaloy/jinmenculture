A Director’s Approach

Wang Su approaches a production through its story, characters and the audience's emotions. Atmosphere, suspense and stage rhythm work together, with magic and illusions forming part of the theatrical language.

Drawing on 18 years in magic and his experience in illusion engineering, he brings a distinctive perspective to directing. The creative and engineering teams test whether an idea is achievable, then adapt it to the venue, performers and budget.

COLLABORATION GUIDE

Before We Begin,
Questions & Answers.

Project types, scope, production method and quotations.

01

Which projects suit this service?

Concerts, tours, brand launches, corporate galas, television, cultural festivals, tourism productions and original immersive projects.

02

Can you direct a full show or contribute to one part?

Yes. We can provide creative and scene consultancy, develop and direct a complete show or theatre production, or shape the illusions within an existing production. We agree the scope according to the project's stage, team and needs.

03

Do you also design and build the apparatus?

Yes. Jinmen combines creative direction with illusion engineering, including structural design, fabrication, testing, performer training and site adaptation.

04

How do I request a quotation?

Share the project type, date, city, venue, audience size, budget range and desired stage moment so we can define the scope.
