We Create Our Own Worlds

From Original Story

Shanghai Jinmen Culture Media Co., Ltd. develops original immersive show and theatre IP in Shanghai. Our process combines concept development, scriptwriting, show direction, cast training, illusion engineering, technical testing and venue adaptation within one creative system.

We are currently developing two original projects: a residency-format immersive production that has completed four test performances, and a completed original script seeking production partners. We welcome co-production, residency and licensing discussions with producers, investors, theatres, commercial venues and cultural tourism projects.

Ghost of the Circus

Copyright-Registered Literary Work

Copyright Registration No.: 国作登字-2025-A-SZ00541636
Copyright holder: Shanghai Jinmen Culture Media Co., Ltd.

A 90-minute original immersive magic mystery show designed for five core performers, an audience of 100–150 and a venue of approximately 200–300 square metres. Four test performances have been completed in Shanghai. Its story, script, performance structure, illusion devices and stage mechanisms were originally developed by Jinmen Culture.

Performance history: four approved commercial performances were presented at Shimao Plaza · Star Space in Shanghai from 31 October to 2 November 2025. Administrative approval reference: 黄文旅许〔2025〕1745号.
