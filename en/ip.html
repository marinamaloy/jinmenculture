Shanghai Jinmen Culture Media Co., Ltd. develops original immersive show and theatre IP in Shanghai. Our process combines concept development, scriptwriting, show direction, cast training, illusion engineering, technical testing and venue adaptation within one creative system.

We are developing three original projects: Ghost of the Circus has completed four trial performances, while It Is Watching and FOUR404 Interactive Show have completed scripts. We welcome immersive theatre partnerships with producers, investors, theatres, commercial venues and cultural tourism projects, including co-production in China, resident productions and show licensing.

Ghost of the Circus

Step inside an old theatre, where a performer suddenly dies as the show begins. The theatre owner, an illusionist, investigates the mysterious death. From their seats, guests draw closer to the mystery through conversation, questions and moments of magic.

A 90-minute original immersive magic mystery with five core performers, initially designed for 100–150 guests in approximately 200–300 m². Venue requirements are open to discussion. Four trial performances have been completed in Shanghai; the project is seeking partners and investors.

It Is Watching

A couple move into a new home, and strange things begin to happen. A two-storey house takes centre stage as magic and illusion effects bring the unexplained to life before the audience. Is it a ghost, or is something else behind it all? The truth is revealed at the end of the story.

An original psychological mystery and illusion-led theatre project with a completed script, seeking producers, investors, theatres and resident venue partners.

FOUR404 Interactive Show

An original nightlife show combining social interaction and illusion, with a completed script. Four characters with different abilities draw guests into an experience shaped by connections between strangers, relationship choices, live interaction, dance, illusion design and AI-driven psychological mechanisms. Guests actively participate and form real connections with one another.

The project brings together meeting new people, entertainment, visual spectacle and moments worth sharing. Designed for nightlife venues, commercial spaces, unconventional theatres and resident productions, it is seeking producers, investors, theatres, commercial venues and residency partners.

Industry Context & Sources

A national report published in 2025 found that China's immersive performing arts market approached RMB 2 billion in box-office revenue and attracted more than 12 million visits, with audiences aged 25–35 forming its core consumer group.
