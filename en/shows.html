BOOKING GUIDE

Before You Book,
Questions & Answers.

Customization, pricing, availability and booking.

01

How do I book a brand show?

Contact us by WeChat, email or telephone with the date, venue, audience size and event objective.

02

Can the show be customized?

Yes. We can develop content, interaction and visual effects around your company or brand goals. If you do not have a specific brief, an established programme can be selected to suit the occasion.

03

What are the reference prices?

The 15-minute show costs RMB 2,000 in Shanghai; other locations are quoted separately. Reference options start at RMB 3,500 for 45 minutes and up to 20 guests, or RMB 5,500 for 60 minutes and up to 32 guests. Company-specific content and other custom requirements are confirmed separately.

04

How far in advance should I book?

We normally recommend 2–4 weeks' notice; established shows may be confirmed faster when dates are available.
