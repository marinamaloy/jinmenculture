Jinmen Culture Shanghai:
Immersive Shows & Illusion Engineering

Shanghai Jinmen Culture Media Co., Ltd. is a Shanghai-based immersive show production and illusion engineering company working across original show IP, physical illusion devices, custom brand performances and creative direction. In an age of digital excess, we believe the moments that move people most are the ones they witness but cannot explain.

From close-up magic to large-scale immersive productions, and from commercial venues to brand events, we draw on 18 years of magic and illusion experience to turn the impossible into something audiences witness in person. Digital media may support the story, while our core capability is translating ideas into physical illusions that can be built, tested and performed.

Our mission is to create moments that audiences cannot explain and cannot forget. These moments need no screen, no post-production — only a real person and a meticulously designed illusion.

Before We Begin,
Questions & Answers

Services, locations and how a project begins.

What services does Jinmen Culture provide?

Original immersive shows, illusion engineering and device design, custom brand performances, creative direction, co-production and IP partnerships.

Where are you based and where do you work?

We are based in Shanghai and work with brands, theatres, commercial venues, production companies and tourism projects across China.

Who founded Jinmen Culture?

Founder Wang Su is the artistic director and chief illusion engineer, with 18 years of experience and the Merlin Award for Best Magic Device Design.

How does a project begin?
