Jinmen Culture Shanghai:
Immersive Shows & Illusion Engineering

Shanghai Jinmen Culture Media Co., Ltd. is a Shanghai-based immersive show production and stage illusion design company. We create original theatre, custom performances and physical illusion devices.

We bring creative development and engineering together, from storytelling, direction and effect design to fabrication, testing and live performance. Each idea is developed around the practical needs of a production.

We work with theatres, brands and commercial venues. Founder Wang Su brings 18 years of experience in magic and illusion creation, informing how we develop projects for live audiences.

Before We Begin,
Questions & Answers

Services, locations and how a project begins.

What services does Jinmen Culture provide?

Original immersive shows, illusion engineering and device design, custom brand performances, creative direction, co-production and IP partnerships.

Where are you based and where do you work?

We are based in Shanghai and work with brands, theatres, commercial venues, production companies and tourism projects across China.

Who founded Jinmen Culture?

Jinmen Culture founder Wang Su is a show director, artistic director and chief illusion engineer. He has 18 years of experience in magic and illusion creation and received the Merlin Award for Best Magic Device Design.

How does a project begin?

Share the project type, venue, timing, audience size and budget range. We begin with a briefing followed by creative and technical assessment.
