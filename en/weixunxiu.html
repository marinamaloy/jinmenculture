BOOKING GUIDE

Before the Show,
Questions & Answers.

Venues, audience size, languages and booking.

01

Where is Weixunxiu suitable?

It suits bars, boutique hotels, corporate dinners, private parties and brand events, with interaction adapted to each venue.

02

What is the duration and audience capacity?

The trial package is 45 minutes for up to 20 guests; the standard package is 60 minutes for up to 32 guests. Final arrangements depend on the venue and agreed programme.

03

How do I book Weixunxiu?

Use the booking form on this page or contact us by WeChat Alex-lives, email 1379197706@qq.com or telephone +86 136 4170 9150.

04

Is the show available in English?

Yes. The show can be performed in Chinese, English or bilingually for international companies and audiences.

05

What makes Weixunxiu different?

It combines close-up magic, social interaction and venue atmosphere, helping guests connect through the performance.

06

Can we choose the date and discuss regular cooperation?

Yes. Any day of the week can be discussed. For regular cooperation, we agree the dates, frequency, performance fee and any revenue share together; the two-shows-per-week plan over two months or more is a reference option.
