上海浸门文化传媒有限公司在上海开发原创沉浸式演出与戏剧IP。我们的工作覆盖概念开发、剧本创作、导演设计、演员训练、幻觉工程、技术测试与场地适配，使故事、表演和舞台机关从项目开始阶段便形成统一的创作系统。

Shanghai Jinmen Culture Media Co., Ltd. develops original immersive show and theatre IP in Shanghai. Our process combines concept development, scriptwriting, show direction, cast training, illusion engineering, technical testing and venue adaptation within one creative system.

我们目前正在推进两个原创项目：一个已完成四场测试演出的驻场沉浸式作品，以及一个已完成剧本、正在寻找制作合作伙伴的新项目。我们欢迎制作人、投资人、剧场、商业场馆及文旅项目洽谈联合制作、驻演与授权合作。

We are currently developing two original projects: a residency-format immersive production that has completed four test performances, and a completed original script seeking production partners. We welcome co-production, residency and licensing discussions with producers, investors, theatres, commercial venues and cultural tourism projects.

行业背景与参考资料

INDUSTRY CONTEXT & SOURCES

根据2025年发布的全国沉浸式演艺专项报告，中国沉浸式演艺总票房规模接近20亿元，观演人数突破1200万人次；25至35岁观众构成消费主力。

A national report published in 2025 found that China's immersive performing arts market approached RMB 2 billion in box-office revenue and attracted more than 12 million visits, with audiences aged 25–35 forming its core consumer group.

— Guangming Daily · 全国首个沉浸式演艺专项报告发布

上海市文化和旅游局报道显示，截至2022年12月，上海版《不眠之夜》已演出超过1500场，累计观众超过45万人次；上海原创沉浸式戏剧IP也正在进入长期驻演与商业空间。

According to the Shanghai Municipal Administration of Culture and Tourism, the Shanghai production of Sleep No More had exceeded 1,500 performances and attracted more than 450,000 visitors by December 2022, while original immersive theatre IP was expanding into residency and commercial-space formats.

— 上海市文化和旅游局 · 上海沉浸式演艺从一枝独秀到遍地开花

文化和旅游部报道的上海原创音乐剧版权海外输出案例表明，原创演出IP不仅可以进行现场演出，也可以通过版权授权、区域合作与国际制作实现进一步开发。

A Shanghai case reported by China's Ministry of Culture and Tourism demonstrates how original performance IP can extend beyond live staging through copyright licensing, regional partnerships and international production.

— 中华人民共和国文化和旅游部 · 上海原创演出版权输出
