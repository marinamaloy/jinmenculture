幻觉工程包括哪些服务？What does illusion engineering include?

包括舞台幻觉设计、定制机关与装置、结构与安全评估、制作测试、演员训练和现场适配。

Stage illusion design, custom mechanisms and devices, structural and safety assessment, fabrication, testing, performer training and site adaptation.

如何讨论原创IP或投资合作？How do we discuss original IP or investment cooperation?

请说明希望参与的项目、合作身份、地区、资源条件和预算范围，我们会据此安排进一步沟通。

Tell us the project, proposed role, territory, available resources and budget range so we can arrange the next discussion.

是否可以定制品牌演出？Can you create a custom brand show?

可以。我们会根据品牌信息、活动目标、观众、场地和预算定制演出与幻觉环节。

Yes. We develop performances and illusion sequences around the brand message, event objective, audience, venue and budget.

通常多久可以收到回复？How quickly will I receive a reply?

通常会在一个工作日内回复；复杂项目可能需要进一步了解资料后才能给出初步范围。

We normally reply within one business day. Complex projects may require additional information before we can outline the initial scope.
