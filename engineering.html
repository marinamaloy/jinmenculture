常见问题

Before We Build,
Questions & Answers.

关于装置类型、制作周期、价格与项目支持。

Equipment types, production schedules, pricing and project support.

01

你们提供哪些类型的幻觉装置？

What types of illusion equipment do you provide?

我们设计科技互动效果、大型人物出现与消失装置、悬浮系统、舞台机关及辅助剧情效果，并可根据项目定制。

We design interactive effects, large-scale appearance and disappearance devices, levitation systems, stage mechanisms and supporting story effects.

02

定制装置需要多长时间？

How long does a custom build take?

周期取决于规模、技术复杂度、测试要求和场地条件。完成初步评估后，我们会提供具体制作时间表。

Timing depends on scale, technical complexity, testing and venue conditions. A detailed schedule follows the initial assessment.

03

如何确定价格？

How is pricing determined?

目录价格可作为参考；定制项目根据结构、材料、控制方式、数量、运输和现场服务范围报价。

Catalogue prices are indicative. Custom quotations reflect structure, materials, controls, quantity, transport and on-site services.

04

是否提供安装与技术支持？

Do you provide installation and support?

可根据项目提供现场安装、装置调试、操作培训及后续技术支持，具体范围会写入项目方案。

On-site installation, calibration, training and follow-up support can be included and documented in the proposal.
