为什么选择浸门文化？

Why Choose Jinmen Culture?

浸门文化是一家位于上海、服务中国市场的沉浸式演出制作与幻觉工程公司。 我们将戏剧创作、魔术设计、机械工程与现场运营结合，为品牌、剧场、 文旅项目和商业场馆打造真实可落地的沉浸式体验。

Jinmen Culture is a Shanghai-based immersive show production and illusion engineering company serving clients across China. We combine theatre, magic design, physical engineering and live operations to create practical, venue-ready experiences for brands, theatres, cultural attractions and commercial spaces.

我们的项目经验包括开心麻花舞台幻觉机关、SMG电视综艺魔术设计、 UMEPLAY密室特效装置、博物馆文旅装置与邮轮沉浸式演出。

Our experience includes stage illusion systems for Mahua FunAge, television magic design for SMG, escape-room effects for UMEPLAY, museum installations and immersive cruise productions.

浸门文化致力于将不可能变为现实。无论是为品牌打造沉浸式体验，还是为场馆设计幻觉装置，我们的目标始终如一：创造让观众难忘、让客户满意的独特体验。

Jinmen Culture is committed to turning the impossible into reality. Whether creating immersive experiences for brands or designing illusion devices for venues, our goal remains the same: to create unique experiences that audiences remember and clients love.

关于沉浸式演出、幻觉工程与定制项目合作。

Immersive productions, illusion engineering and custom creative partnerships.

01 浸门文化提供哪些服务？ What services does Jinmen Culture provide? +

我们提供沉浸式演出制作、幻觉工程、舞台魔术装置、品牌定制演出、原创IP开发与演艺创意导演服务。

We provide immersive show production, illusion engineering, custom stage illusion systems, branded performances, original IP development and creative direction.

02 浸门文化的服务范围在哪里？ Where does Jinmen Culture operate? +

我们位于上海，为中国各地的品牌、剧场、商业场馆、文旅项目与制作公司提供创意、设计、制作和现场支持。

We are based in Shanghai and work with brands, theatres, commercial venues, cultural attractions and production companies throughout China.

03 可以定制企业活动或品牌魔术演出吗？ Can you create a custom show for a corporate event? +

可以。我们会根据活动主题、场地条件、观众人数、品牌信息与预算，定制沉浸式魔术演出、互动环节和视觉幻觉效果。

Yes. We tailor immersive magic performances, interactive sequences and visual illusion effects to the event theme, venue, audience size, brand message and production budget.

04 是否提供幻觉装置的设计与制作？ Does Jinmen design and build illusion equipment? +

是的。我们为剧场、商业空间、密室、文旅项目和品牌活动，设计制作定制幻觉装置、舞台机关与物理特效系统。

Yes. We design and build custom illusion devices, stage mechanisms and physical effects for theatres, commercial venues, escape rooms, cultural attractions and brand events.

05 如何开始一个定制项目？ How do we start a custom project? +

请通过微信、电子邮件或电话联系我们，并提供项目类型、场地、时间、观众规模和预算范围。我们会据此安排初步沟通与创意评估。

Contact us by WeChat, email or telephone with the project type, venue, schedule, audience capacity and approximate budget. We will arrange an initial consultation and creative assessment.
