What is Weixunxiu? Immersive magic experience in Shanghai

微醺秀是专为酒吧、Homebar、精品酒店和社交活动设计的沉浸式互动交友近景魔术表演在上海。在一个普通的夜晚，你的场馆将变成一个充满惊喜与暧昧还有笑声的空间。观众不是在看表演——他们在通过这场秀来链接陌生人。

Weixunxiu is an immersive close-up magic social show for bars, boutique hotels and social events in Shanghai. On an ordinary evening, your venue transforms into a space full of surprise and wonder.
